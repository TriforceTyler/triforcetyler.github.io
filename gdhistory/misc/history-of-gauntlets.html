History of GD Gauntlets

This page documents the history of Gauntlets in GD. This will list when each Gauntlet was added, and times when Gauntlet levels were actually changed (ex. Spike Gauntlet level 1).

Since Gauntlets are server-side, they can be added without waiting for an update, which is why they can be periodically released.

During Update 2.1

  • Gauntlets were first added on January 17, 2017 (January 19th for Android and iOS). There were initially no Gauntlets available.
  • The Fire and Ice Gauntlets, the first 2 Gauntlets in the game, were added on February 5th, 2017.
  • The Poison and Shadow Gauntlets were added the day after the first 2 on February 6th.
  • The Lava and Bonus Gauntlets were then added the day after that, on February 7th.
