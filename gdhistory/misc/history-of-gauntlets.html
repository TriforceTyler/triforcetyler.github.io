History of GD Gauntlets

This page documents the history of Gauntlets in GD. This will list when each Gauntlet was added, and times when Gauntlet levels were actually changed (ex. Spike Gauntlet level 1).

Since Gauntlets are server-side, they can be added without waiting for an update, which is why they can be periodically released.

During Update 2.1

  • Gauntlets were first added on January 17, 2017 (January 19th for Android and iOS). There were initially no Gauntlets available.
  • The Fire and Ice Gauntlets, the first 2 Gauntlets in the game, were added on February 5th, 2017.
  • The Poison and Shadow Gauntlets were added the day after the first 2 on February 6th.
  • The Lava and Bonus Gauntlets were then added the day after that, on February 7th.
  • The final 2 Gauntlets from 2.1 were the Chaos and Demon Gauntlets, added on February 8th. These two Gauntlets are also the Gauntlets related to the Demon Guardian.

During Update 2.11

  • The Crystal Gauntlet was added with the release of 2.11 on November 16th, 2017.
  • The Doom Gauntlet was then added 2 days afterwards, on November 18th. This was released following the conclusion of the Doom Gauntlet Contest.
  • Two more Gauntlets were added on December 28th, 2017. These were the Magic and Spike Gauntlets.
  • On March 28th, 2018, another Gauntlet was added: the Time Gauntlet.
  • The Monster and Death Gauntlets were added the month afterward on April 11th, 2018. These were the final Gauntlets of 2.11. The Monster Gauntlet was added because the Monster Gauntlet Contest had concluded.

During Update 2.2

  • 5 Gauntlets were added with the release of 2.2 on December 19th, 2023, after over 5 years with no Gauntlet releases. These were the World, Galaxy, Universe, Discord, and Split Gauntlets.
  • The bug where the last few Gauntlets all had gray backgrounds on all non-Steam versions was fixed with the release of 2.2 on December 20th, 2023.
  • The small mistake where the name of the Spike Gauntlet wasn't capitalized was also fixed with the release of 2.2.
  • The first Gauntlet released after 2.2, the Castle Gauntlet, was released on January 25th, 2024. This is also the first of the 30 teased 2.2 Gauntlets that was added to the game.
  • The Portal Gauntlet was released on March 3rd, 2024.
  • The Water Gauntlet was added to the game on March 18th, 2024.
  • On April 11th, 2024, the Strange Gauntlet released.
  • The Fantasy Gauntlet was added on May 5th, 2024.
  • Later that month, the Cursed Gauntlet was released on May 19th.
  • Another Gauntlet, the Mystery Gauntlet, was released on June 20th, 2024.
  • The Forest Gauntlet was released on July 18th.
  • The Cyborg Gauntlet was added on August 16th.
  • The Inferno Gauntlet was released on September 12th.
  • The NCS I and NCS II Gauntlets were released on October 5th, following the conclusion of the NCS Gauntlet Contest.
  • The Halloween and Haunted Gauntlets were released on Halloween 2024 (October 31st, 2024).
  • The Force Gauntlet was added on December 2nd, 2024.
  • The Christmas Gauntlet was released on Christmas Day 2024 (December 25th, 2024).
  • The first Gauntlet of 2025, the Power Gauntlet, was released on January 26th, 2025.
  • The Space and Cosmos Gauntlets were added on April 5th, 2025, after the conclusion of the Space Gauntlet Contest.
  • The Treasure Gauntlet was released on June 17th, 2025.
  • The Gem Gauntlet was added on September 1st, 2025.
  • The Spooky Gauntlet was released the day after Halloween, on November 1st, 2025.
  • The Random and Chance Gauntlets were released on January 19th, 2026, on the same day as 2.208 and following the conclusion of the Random Gauntlet Contest.
  • The Love Gauntlet was added on February 13th, 2026, right before Valentine's Day.
  • The Acid Gauntlet was released on May 8th, 2026.
  • The Spider Gauntlet was released on July 14th, 2026.
