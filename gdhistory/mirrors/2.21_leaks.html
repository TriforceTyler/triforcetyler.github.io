Information

2.21 will add new features, mainly delayed 2.2 features, that include the Map, and new official platformer levels.

The Map will have selected user-created levels, spread out into different areas and themes. It will also have lots of hidden secrets, chests, shops, Demon Towers, and more.

Other features added in 2.21 will be custom keybinds, improvements to Autobuild, and ways to unlock all 2.2 icons that cannot be unlocked in 2.2.

The list of new features in Update 2.209 is long. Update 2.209 also does not include new triggers or gameplay content.

POSSIBLE new Keymaster dialogue for The Map (possibly, since it was seen in the 2.2 Beta GDPS): Where do you think you are going? / One does not simply WALK... into The Map. FIRST! You must complete... THE TOWER!

A new free game will be released after Update 2.21 and before Update 2.3.

Quote from RobTop in a live stream: "One million orbs, you can unlock the rolling spider, or I'll improve it, and hide it on the map, like in the store ...You guys are too easy to troll. No, im not going to make it 1 Million orbs, It's gonna cost like 2 Million Orbs, Maybe 3."

You can kill monsters in the map by tapping on them.

A new string was added into the game files for level completions called "theMapCompletions".

Explorers might not be in 2.21 (if it isn't, then it'd be safe to assume it'd be in 2.3 and would become the main level added in 2.3).

The Explorers song already exists in GD (song #23) but can only be obtained via editing your level's song data.
